Residential Construction

Custom Residential Construction Services

At Rodwealth Construction, we build homes that reflect the individuality and aspirations of the people who will live in them. From custom single-family homes and luxury residences to home additions and multi-family projects, our team combines craftsmanship, attention to detail, and transparent communication to deliver results that exceed expectations.

Building a home is one of the most significant investments a person will make. Our approach centers on close collaboration with homeowners throughout every phase — from the first design consultation to the final walkthrough — ensuring that every decision reflects your vision and every detail meets our rigorous quality standards.

Our Capabilities

Custom Homes

Fully customized single-family homes built from the ground up to your exact specifications, preferences, and lifestyle requirements.

Luxury Residences

High-end residential construction featuring premium finishes, architectural details, and bespoke craftsmanship for discerning homeowners.

Home Additions

Seamlessly integrated additions — including room expansions, second-story additions, and accessory dwelling units — that enhance livability and home value.

Multi-Family Projects

Duplexes, townhomes, and small multi-family developments built with the same attention to detail and quality that defines our single-family work.

Our Methodology

1

Design Consultation

We begin by listening — understanding your goals, lifestyle needs, aesthetic preferences, and budget to ensure the design direction is aligned before any construction begins.

  • Owner goal-setting and vision alignment
  • Design and architect coordination
  • Budget development and feasibility review
2

Construction Planning

Once plans are finalized, we develop a detailed construction schedule, procure materials and subcontractors, and secure all required permits to position the project for a smooth start.

  • Permit acquisition and approvals
  • Material selections and procurement
  • Subcontractor coordination and scheduling
3

Build Execution

Our crews construct your home with care, maintaining ongoing communication with you throughout the build and providing regular progress updates so you are always informed and confident.

  • Foundation, framing, and structural work
  • MEP systems installation and coordination
  • Interior and exterior finishing trades
4

Final Walkthrough

Before handover, we conduct a detailed walkthrough with you to review every element of the finished home, address any items, and ensure you are completely satisfied with the result.

  • Comprehensive punch-list and quality review
  • Final inspections and certificate of occupancy
  • Homeowner orientation and warranty handover
