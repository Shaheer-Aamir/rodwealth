Remodeling

Residential & Commercial Remodeling Services

At Rodwealth Construction, we breathe new life into existing spaces through thoughtful, high-quality remodeling work. Whether you're updating a kitchen, transforming a bathroom, refreshing a commercial interior, or reconfiguring a floor plan entirely, our team brings creativity, craftsmanship, and careful project management to every renovation.

Remodeling an occupied or previously built space requires a different kind of discipline than ground-up construction. Our team excels at working within existing conditions, minimizing disruption to your daily life or business operations, and delivering finishes that look and feel like they were always meant to be there.

Our Capabilities

Kitchen Renovations

Full kitchen remodels including cabinet replacement, countertop installation, appliance integration, plumbing updates, and lighting improvements that transform the heart of your home.

Bathroom Remodeling

Complete bathroom renovations — from tile and fixtures to vanities and shower enclosures — designed for both aesthetic appeal and lasting functionality.

Interior Upgrades

Flooring replacement, trim work, door and window upgrades, painting, and finish improvements that refresh the look and feel of any residential or commercial interior.

Space Reconfiguration

Structural modifications, wall removals, and floor plan updates that optimize how a space functions — creating open layouts or defined rooms to suit changing needs.

Our Methodology

1

Consultation & Design

We begin with an in-depth consultation to understand your goals, assess the existing space, and develop a clear scope of work that aligns your vision with a realistic budget and timeline.

  • On-site assessment and existing condition review
  • Design direction and material selection guidance
  • Detailed scope and budget development
2

Demolition & Preparation

We carefully remove existing finishes, fixtures, and structural elements as required, taking precautions to protect surrounding areas and prepare the space cleanly for new work.

  • Selective demolition and debris removal
  • Subfloor, wall, and ceiling preparation
  • Utility rough-in modifications as needed
3

Renovation & Installation

Our skilled tradespeople install new finishes, fixtures, and systems with precision and care, following a coordinated sequence that keeps the project moving efficiently toward completion.

  • Finish installation — tile, flooring, cabinetry, and trim
  • Fixture and appliance installation
  • Painting, lighting, and final MEP connections
4

Final Inspection

We walk through the completed renovation with you to review every detail, confirm all work meets our quality standards, and ensure the finished space matches your original vision.

  • Detailed punch-list walkthrough with client
  • Final clean and site restoration
  • Project documentation and warranty information
