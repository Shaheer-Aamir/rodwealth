Turnkey Land Development

Turnkey Land Development Services

At Rodwealth Construction, we deliver end-to-end land development solutions — from large-scale civil infrastructure to complete underground utility systems — under one coordinated team. Whether you're developing a residential community, commercial corridor, or mixed-use site, we manage every element of the process with precision, accountability, and a commitment to long-term performance.

Our turnkey approach eliminates the complexity of managing multiple contractors across overlapping scopes. From roadwork and earthwork to water, sewer, and storm drainage installation, our experienced project managers and field crews keep every phase aligned so your site is delivered on schedule, within budget, and fully ready for vertical construction.

Our Capabilities

Road Construction

Full-service roadway construction including subbase preparation, asphalt paving, curbing, and line marking for public and private projects.

Earthwork Operations

Large-scale grading, cut-and-fill operations, compaction, and mass excavation to shape terrain for infrastructure development.

Water & Sewer Systems

Installation of water mains, service laterals, fire hydrants, gravity sewer mains, force mains, and manholes to municipal specifications and tested for full compliance.

Storm Drainage & Utilities

Storm sewer pipe, culverts, detention basins, inlets, and coordinated underground utility networks installed to manage water flow and support long-term site performance.

Our Methodology

1

Planning & Engineering Review

We begin by thoroughly reviewing civil engineering plans, utility alignment sheets, and project specifications to identify conflicts, confirm material requirements, and develop a unified execution strategy across all scopes.

  • Engineering plan review and constructability analysis
  • Utility conflict identification and sequencing
  • Permitting, regulatory coordination, and scheduling
2

Earthwork & Site Preparation

Our crews execute mass earthwork operations with precision, managing cut-and-fill volumes, compaction requirements, and subgrade preparation before utility installation begins.

  • Mass grading, excavation, and compaction testing
  • Erosion and sediment control installation
  • Underground locating and potholing
3

Infrastructure & Utility Installation

We build roadways, drainage structures, and all underground utility systems in a coordinated sequence — eliminating conflicts between trades and keeping the project on schedule.

  • Roadway base, paving, and civil structures
  • Water, sewer, and storm drainage installation
  • Pressure testing, CCTV inspection, and compliance verification
4

Final Inspection & Site Handover

Prior to closeout, our team coordinates all final inspections, municipal acceptance processes, and compliance sign-offs to deliver a fully construction-ready site.

  • Final grade, surface, and utility verification
  • Municipal inspection and acceptance coordination
  • As-built documentation and project closeout
